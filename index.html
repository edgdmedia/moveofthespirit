About

More Than A Conference.
A Move.

Holyghost Meeting is a gathering designed to enable believers to walk practically and consistently in the inheritance reserved for them in Christ — through the ministry, fellowship, and operations of the Holy Spirit. Throughout these meetings, saints will be instructed, strengthened, activated, and equipped to participate more fully in the purposes of God.

What to Expect

01

Spiritual Understanding

Greater clarity concerning the Kingdom of God and the ministry of the Holy Spirit in the life of every believer.

02

Activation & Impartation

Ministry gifts stirred, activated, and strengthened. An atmosphere where believers encounter God's Word with power.

03

Practical Kingdom Living

Leave equipped to walk consistently in the realities that belong to you in Christ — not just spiritually positioned, but practically empowered.

04

Renewed Hunger

Many come tired. Many come with questions. Our prayer is that all leave with a fresh and deepened hunger for the things of God.

This Meeting Is For You If...

You Hunger For More

You know there is more in God and desire a deeper walk with Him.

You Serve In Ministry

Whether pastor, leader, worker, or volunteer — you desire fresh strength.

You Want Clarity

You are trusting God for wisdom, understanding, and alignment for your next steps.

You Long For Community

You value healthy spiritual community, divine relationships, and Kingdom partnerships.

"Come hungry. Come expectant. Come ready to participate in the move of the Spirit."

Testimonies

Evidence of the Move

Photo

After I lost my job, I trusted God for another and joined an organisation as a Technical Team Lead. My confirmation was delayed twice. On the 3rd day of HGM, God ministered through Pastor Tolu to plug our situations into God's power. I boldly said "I plug my confirmation into the power of God". At a meeting with top management, all managers gave positive feedback but the HR head said otherwise. I came out and said "GOD, IT IS NOW YOUR TURN". To the glory of God, the first email I got the next day was my confirmation — a congratulatory email. God did it!

— Brother Damilola

Photo

On day 2 of HGM, I came to church with a severe pain around my left rib. I was so uncomfortable I couldn't sit upright and had to lay down in the car. To God be the glory, immediately after the rejoicing, the pain subsided drastically and I was able to sit upright. Now the pain is completely gone!

— Sister Modupe

Photo

A Sunday before HGM, the pastor prayed for me and said "You are help by God". On the 2nd of January, that miraculous help came in a huge surprise that changed my business life. It is indeed helped by God — a year of shining light.

— Brother Seyi

Photo

Last year I had an issue with my landlord. I prayed and believed God to resolve it, and had a peace that sincerely did not make sense. During HGM, God miraculously changed my landlord's mind which I thought was impossible.

— Sister Funmi

Photo

Reverend gave a specific instruction during HGM August 2023 for those having a career shift to take up certifications to build capacity for the increase coming in the new year. I summoned the courage to take some courses and God helped me overcome many limitations. I completed them and was awarded certificates by December. On Christmas Day 2023, I got a job offer in line with the courses I took with an awesome pay. During HGM 2024, I asked God for His help for the interviews and God favoured me. I have received help from God!

— Brother Emmanuel

Privacy Notice

When you register for Holyghost Meetings, volunteer, or submit a testimony, you may be directed to Google Forms operated by Noble House Christian Centre. Information you provide — such as your name, email address, phone number, church affiliation, and responses to form questions — is collected solely to:

  • Process your registration and communicate event details
  • Coordinate volunteer assignments and ministry support
  • Receive and share testimonies as approved
  • Send relevant updates about the meetings and livestreams

Your data is handled by authorised church personnel and is not sold to third parties. Google Forms is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. For questions or to request removal of your information, contact us at hgm@noblehousecc.org or info@noblehousecc.org.
